title: "Near Wall Removed: The Compartments Laid Bare"
caption: Car No. 7 in section, near wall removed.
---

With the trackside wall cut away, every compartment can be read at once. Our
readers are invited to draw their own conclusions.

Presented in section, the coach reads as a row of small rooms, each with its own
account of the night, and no two accounts agreeing.

The Gazette offers no verdict. It offers the plan of the carriage, the hour of
each movement, and the names of those aboard.
