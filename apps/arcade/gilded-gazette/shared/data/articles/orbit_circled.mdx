A circuit of the carriage made from the trackside disclosed several particulars not apparent from within.

One window stood unlatched. 
Beneath it, on the rain-darkened paintwork, our photographer found the distinct mark of a hand. 
Farther along, a lamp still burned behind the curtain of a berth whose occupant insisted it had been extinguished before midnight.

A full turn about the coach reveals a window unlatched from within and a lamp
still burning in a berth its occupant swears was dark.

From the trackside the coach gives up what its corridors hide: one window
unlatched, and the mark of a hand below it on the paintwork.

The railway declines to say whether the train was stopped between halts. Our
photographer, who was standing beside it, says it was.
