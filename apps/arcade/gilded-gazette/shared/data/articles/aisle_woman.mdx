title: Mysterious Woman Seen Walking the Aisle
caption: The aisle of Car No. 7, looking forward.
---

Railway employees report unusual activity shortly after midnight, and the
steward is unable to account for a second setting laid at table nine.

The steward maintains that the fourth chair was drawn out before the soup, and
that no one sat in it. 

> "I did not lay it," he told our correspondent. "But the chair was drawn out before the soup came, as though someone had already taken her seat."

A porter questioned afterward disputed the steward’s account, but became visibly uneasy when asked whether he had seen anyone in the aisle.

> "I saw somebody," he said. "That is not the same thing."

He would say no more.

Several passengers later reported seeing a woman walking alone toward the forward vestibule shortly after midnight. 
No description agrees beyond this: she wore dark clothing, moved without haste, and was not seen returning.


Passengers were thereafter asked to remain in their compartments until the border and to report any door found unlocked that ought not to have been.
