title: The Continental Express Departs Under Cover of Rain
caption: The platform, moments before departure.

The Continental Express departed through a hard evening rain, the platform heavy with the smell of wet timber, musky tobacco, coal smoke, and hot iron.
Glisening, the lamps were already burning against the weather and the station nearly deserted by the time the last whistle sounded.


Boarding was otherwise without incident, save for two small irregularities which railway officials have thus far been unable to explain: one berth appears to have been claimed by two passengers, while a trunk was found aboard which no porter remembers carrying.
Our correspondent, travelling in Car No. 7, observed the guard make two complete examinations of the train after departure. On the second occasion he was heard asking a steward, in some irritation,

> "Then where is the other one?"

The steward gave no answer within our hearing.

At the last halt, the stationmaster declined to discuss the matter and ordered the platform cleared before the train resumed its journey.
Railway officials continue their enquiries. Any passenger possessing knowledge of the disputed berth, the unidentified luggage, or the person to whom either may belong is requested to present themselves to the conductor before the border crossing.


Passengers boarded without incident, though the guard reports one berth claimed
twice and a trunk no porter admits to carrying.

Our correspondent, travelling in Car No. 7, reports that the guard has twice
walked the length of the train without satisfying himself as to the whereabouts
of the missing party. The stationmaster at the last halt declined to comment.

Enquiries continue. Any passenger with knowledge of the affair is asked to
present themselves at the conductor's office before the border crossing.
