The conductor, with a face of confusion, insisted the lamps were trimmed at eight.

Yet no passenger admits to having passed that way since, and the corridor now lies dark from vestibule to vestibule.

Our correspondent walked its length twice and met no one, which he notes is itself remarkable on a train carrying forty-one souls.

As the wind continued to howl, the conductor became increasingly agitated and emotional distraught.

Questions concerning the lamps are now met not with answers, but accusations, curses, and theories implicating nearly every demon aboard.
