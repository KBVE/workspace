Short-hafted, single-bitted, and the one thing in the van that has an honest
reason to be there: a crate is opened with an axe or it is not opened.

## The Bit

Bright along the edge and dulled at the heel, which is wear from prying rather
than from cutting.

## The Haft

Wrapped where a hand goes, and the wrapping is newer than the wear under it.

## Provenance

Railway tool or the Order's, depending on who is asked, and the two answers
have been given by two people about the same axe.
