Tincture of opium, dispensed in Vienna, in a brown bottle with a measure marked
down the side.

## The Measure

The book in the doctor's bag records what was poured and when. The bottle is
three doses lighter than the book has an entry for.

## Who It Is For

The label carries a name that is not Dr. Weiss's and not any name on the
passenger list, in handwriting that is his.

## Provenance

Declared at Calais as medical, which it is, and which is the reason nobody
looked at how much of it there was.
