Webley, service issue, and older than the marriage it came into. Carried in a
travelling case fitted for it, which is not how a keepsake is carried.

## The Cylinder

Six chambers, five loaded. The sixth has been fired and not cleaned since, and
the fouling is old enough to be about some other evening entirely.

## The Case

Lined in green baize with a recess cut for the revolver and a second recess,
the same size, cut for something that is not in it.

## Provenance

Sir Rupert's, entered on no declaration at Calais. Lady Beaumont says she did
not know it was in the luggage, and knows which case it is in.
