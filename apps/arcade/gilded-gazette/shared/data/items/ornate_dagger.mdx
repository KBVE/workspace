A hand's length of blade, chased along the spine and fitted to a sheath it is
no longer in. Both are on the floor of the van, a foot apart, past the crates
and under the window, where the light does not reach.

## The Blade

Ornamental, and sharpened anyway. The chasing runs to the edge and stops, the
way a maker's work does when the edge is meant to be honed and the pattern is
not meant to be worn away.

It has been wiped. Not cleaned -- wiped, once, along one face, by somebody in
more of a hurry than the wiping was worth.

## The Sheath

Lying open and empty, which is the part worth reading. A dagger dropped in a
struggle keeps its sheath on a belt. A dagger drawn somewhere else and carried
here arrives with the sheath in the other hand.

## Provenance

Nothing on the manifest matches it, and nothing in the van was opened to let it
out. It came aboard in somebody's luggage and left that luggage before the
border.
