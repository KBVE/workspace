Plain, short, and issued rather than chosen: one of four the same, worn under the
cloak where a coat would hide it.

## The Blade

No maker's mark and no ornament. The Order does not commission, it equips, and
what it equips is the cheapest thing that will do the work.

## The Sheath

Sewn to the belt rather than hung from it, which is how a thing is carried by
somebody expecting to need it in a corridor.

## Provenance

Not on the manifest. Nothing the escort carries is on the manifest, and the
warrant is why nobody at Calais asked.
