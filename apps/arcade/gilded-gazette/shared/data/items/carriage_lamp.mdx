Brass, weighted in the base so it stands when the car sways, and heavy enough
in the hand that nobody carries one further than the next bracket.

## The Base

A dent in the rim that the company's fitter did not put there, and a smear
inside the dent that has been wiped at rather than cleaned.

## The Bracket

Every lamp on the train sits in a bracket, and the brackets are numbered. One
lamp is in a bracket that is not the one stamped on it.

## Provenance

Railway property again, in a train full of it. The guard turns them down at ten
and does not count them, because in thirty years there has never been a reason
to.
