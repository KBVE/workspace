Small, worn bright at the bow, and stamped with a number that belongs to no
berth in this carriage.

## Markings

Number 14, and this carriage ends at 8. The stamping is old; the wear on the bow
is not.

## Provenance

It was in the player's coat before Calais, and the player does not remember
putting it there.
