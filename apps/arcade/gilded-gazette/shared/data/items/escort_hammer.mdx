A head of steel on a short haft, weighted so far forward that it is useless for
anything except what it is for.

## The Head

Faced on one side and spiked on the other, and the spike has been ground back to
a stub by somebody who wanted it to look like a mallet.

## Why It Is Aboard

Nobody has offered a reason. Dame Marchand answers questions about the cargo
with the same sentence each time, and this is not the cargo.

## Provenance

Under the same warrant as the rest, sealed in wax that is not any nation's.
