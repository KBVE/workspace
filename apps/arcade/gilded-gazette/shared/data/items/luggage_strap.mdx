Six feet of harness leather with a brass buckle, of which the guard's van holds
a dozen and the register accounts for eleven.

## The Leather

Stretched along one section and nowhere else, the way leather goes when it has
been pulled hard once rather than loaded often.

## Where They Hang

On a rail inside the van door, in a row, and the gap in the row is not at the
end where a man reaching in a hurry would take one from.

## Provenance

Railway property, unremarkable, and the only thing aboard that could kill a man
without anybody having brought it.
