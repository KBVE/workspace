A yard of straight steel, which is a yard more than the width of the corridor it
would have to be swung in.

## The Blade

Sharpened along its whole length and lately, by somebody who did the work in a
hurry and left the stone's grit in the fuller.

## Where It Was

Racked in the guard's van with three others, in a stand made for four. The
leather at one station is compressed and at the rest it is not.

## Provenance

The Order's, under warrant, and the only weapon aboard that could not be
concealed by anybody carrying it anywhere.
