Handed aboard at Paris, folded twice, and addressed to a name that is not the
one M. Dupont travels under.

## Text

It reads, in full: DO NOT DINE AT NINE.

## Provenance

Filed at Paris at half past three, an hour before the train left, by a sender
the clerk recorded only as "a lady".

- Filed 15:30, Paris
- No sender name on the counterfoil
- Delivered to the carriage, not the platform office
