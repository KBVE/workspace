The strip is printed in the red the Gazette keeps for losses and warnings, which is the
Order's way of saying this one is not a formality.

## The Rule

Goblins are banned from the Northern Line. They will be slain by law, and the Order is
held blameless in the carrying out of it.

## What Nobody Says

No clause explains how a guard is to tell. In practice the question is settled by
whoever is holding the lamp.
