The full posting, by order of the Knights of the Northern Line, in the frame every
carriage carries beside the third window.

## The Clauses

- Elves not allowed without permits.
- Goblins are banned, and will be slain by law.
- The rear cars are reserved for the older bloods and are locked between stations.
- The Order's word is the record, and the record is not open to reading.

## What Nobody Says

The fourth clause is the one that matters. Everything the other three claim rests on a
record no passenger is permitted to check.
