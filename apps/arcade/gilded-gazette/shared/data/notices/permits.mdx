Posted at Aldermoor and pasted in every carriage before the train left the platform.

## The Rule

Permits are issued at Aldermoor and nowhere else on this line. A permit names one
journey, one direction, and one carriage, and the guard is entitled to ask for it at
any point between stations.

## What Nobody Says

The permit office at Aldermoor keeps no evening hours. Anyone who boards after dark
boards without one.
