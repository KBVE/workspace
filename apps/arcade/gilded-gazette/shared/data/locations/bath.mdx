A bath, a boiler, and a list on the door of the hours it may be had in, none of
which are these hours.

## Appearance

Empty and cold. The boiler is out, the tap has been run recently enough to have
left the basin wet, and nothing else in the room has been touched all night.

## Why It Matters

Nobody has any business here between eleven and six, which makes it the only
room aboard where being found at all is the whole of the answer.
