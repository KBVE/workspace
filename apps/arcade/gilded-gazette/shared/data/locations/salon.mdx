Where the company would like you to sit after dinner, and where nobody sits
alone.

## Appearance

Two tables, four chairs, and a great deal of room between them that the company
charges for. The lamps are turned up higher here than anywhere else aboard.

## Why It Matters

It is the only room on the train designed to be sat in by people who did not
arrive together, which makes it the only room where being seen with somebody
means nothing at all.
