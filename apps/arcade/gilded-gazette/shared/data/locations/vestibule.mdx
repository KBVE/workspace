The joint between cars, where the floor moves under you and conversation is
impossible.

## Appearance

Concertina walls, a handrail worn bright, and a door to the trackside that is
supposed to be locked while the train is moving.

## Why It Matters

The loudest six feet on the train, which makes it the only place aboard where
two people can speak and be certain of not being overheard.
