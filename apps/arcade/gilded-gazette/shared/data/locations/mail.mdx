Sealed at Calais under a different authority to the rest of the train, and
carrying post for three countries.

## Appearance

Sacks in crates, a sorting shelf nobody has used since the border, and a seal on
the far door that is a customs matter rather than a railway one.

## Why It Matters

The one room aboard the guard has no key to. Whatever is in here was put in at
Calais by somebody who does not work for this company, and will be taken out at
Vienna by somebody else who does not.
