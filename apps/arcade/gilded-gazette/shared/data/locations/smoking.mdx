Six seats, a window that opens, and the smell of every journey this carriage has
made since it left the works.

## Appearance

Dark, unlit, and unused tonight. The window is down two inches, which somebody
did and nobody has undone.

## Why It Matters

It is the far end of the train. Everything that happened aboard tonight happened
between here and the guard's van, and nobody had a reason to come this far.
