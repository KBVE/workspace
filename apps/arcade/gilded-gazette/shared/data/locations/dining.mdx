Eight tables, six of them laid, and a steward who remembers what everyone drinks
without being told.

## Appearance

White cloth, brass rail, and the window glass gone black by eight o'clock so the
car dines looking at itself.

## Service

The last order is taken at half past nine and the car is dark by ten. Anyone
still seated after that was not eating.
