Not aboard at all. Where the passengers were before they were passengers, and
the only place the enquiry cannot follow them.

## Appearance

Gas lamps at intervals, a clock two minutes fast, and porters who have already
gone home.

## Why It Matters

Everyone boarded somewhere. What they were carrying when they did is a question
the manifest answers and the passengers do not.
