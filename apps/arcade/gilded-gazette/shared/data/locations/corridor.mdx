Feels about a yard wide, just enough room to avoid a leg cramp but it is a full length of the train.
And the only way from any berth to any other.


## Appearance

Windows down one side, doors down the other, and a runner of carpet that shows
every step taken on it while the rain was falling.

## Why It Matters

Nobody stays in the corridor. Everybody crosses it. Whoever was where, they were
here first.
