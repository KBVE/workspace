Thirty years of this corridor, and the only man aboard who is paid to notice
everything.

## Appearance

The company's dark blue, worn to the shine at the elbows, and a chain of keys
that announces him a carriage before he arrives.

## Alibi

He gives his account the way he marks the register: completely, in order, and
with one thing left out. Thirty years of being the man who noticed is thirty
years of choosing what to say he noticed.

## The Register

Every passenger, every stop, in a copperplate hand that has not changed in
thirty years. Tonight's page has one boarding entered out of order.

## Under Enquiry

He is not a suspect and he knows it, which is why what he chooses not to say is
worth more than anything the passengers volunteer.

## The Body

In the company's dark blue, worn to the shine at the elbows, and the chain of
keys that announces him a carriage before he arrives is silent and still on it.
Every key is there. He has said for thirty years that he holds the only one.

The register is open and the copperplate does not waver, including on the last
line he wrote.
