Travelling without a maid, which the conductor notes and does not explain.

## Appearance

Mourning black of a cut three seasons old, worn as though it were this year's.
A jet brooch at the throat, and gloves she does not remove, not for dinner and
not for the customs officer.

## Alibi

She says she did not leave her compartment after the dinner service, and that
she heard nothing until the guard began knocking on doors.

- Says she retired at nine
- Says the corridor was lit when she last saw it
- Says she has never met Dr. Weiss

## What the Guard Says

Her trunk was carried aboard by no porter anyone can name, and it went into the
van without a label. He is certain of both things and will say neither in front
of her.

## Under Enquiry

The brooch is jet, which is mourning wear. The ring beneath the left glove is
not, and she has worn it since Calais.
