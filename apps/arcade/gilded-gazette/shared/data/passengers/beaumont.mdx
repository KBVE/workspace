Travelling without a maid, which the conductor notes and does not explain.

## Appearance

Mourning black of a cut three seasons old, worn as though it were this year's.
A jet brooch at the throat, and gloves she does not remove, not for dinner and
not for the customs officer.

## Alibi

Whatever she says she was doing, she says it once and does not enlarge on it.
Lady Beaumont has been asked three times now and has given the same length of
answer each time, to the word.

## What the Guard Says

Her trunk was carried aboard by no porter anyone can name, and it went into the
van without a label. He is certain of both things and will say neither in front
of her.

## Under Enquiry

The brooch is jet, which is mourning wear. The ring beneath the left glove is
not, and she has worn it since Calais.

## The Body

Still gloved, which she was not at dinner, and wearing the rings she is said never
to take off. Sir Rupert's case was found closed and latched with the second recess
in it empty, and nobody has said what the second recess was cut for.

Whoever it was, she had turned to face them. Lady Beaumont did not die with her
back to anybody.
