A physician, he says, of Vienna, though his case holds instruments no physician
carries.

## Appearance

Grey at the temples, spectacles he cleans when asked a direct question, and a
coat too heavy for a heated carriage.

## Alibi

He was walking the corridor because he could not sleep, and he offered his help
because he is a doctor and that is what a doctor does.

- Says he slept badly and rose after midnight
- Says he saw no one in the corridor
- Says the lamps were already out when he passed

## The Case

Brass fittings, Viennese maker's mark, and a tray of instruments too fine for
surgery and too sharp for anything else. He does not let a porter carry it.

## Under Enquiry

He asked whether help was wanted before anyone aboard had said that anything was
wrong.
