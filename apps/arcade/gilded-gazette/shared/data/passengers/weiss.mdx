A physician, he says, of Vienna, though his case holds instruments no physician
carries.

## Appearance

Grey at the temples, spectacles he cleans when asked a direct question, and a
coat too heavy for a heated carriage.

## Alibi

A physician's account, given precisely, with the times to the quarter hour and
not one of them rounded. He has clearly thought about how it will sound, which
is not the same as having invented it.

## The Case

Brass fittings, Viennese maker's mark, and a tray of instruments too fine for
surgery and too sharp for anything else. He does not let a porter carry it.

## Under Enquiry

He asked whether help was wanted before anyone aboard had said that anything was
wrong.

## The Body

The bag is beside him, open, and the book in it records what was poured and when
in his own hand up to an entry he did not finish. The pen is capped.

A physician of Vienna knows what is fatal and in what quantity, which is worth
nothing at all to him now and a great deal to whoever is asked about it.
