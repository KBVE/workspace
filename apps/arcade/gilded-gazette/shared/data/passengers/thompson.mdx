A grain factor from Norwich, travelling on business he describes twice and
differently.

## Appearance

A good coat kept too long, boots newly resoled, and a case with a lock that has
been forced and repaired.

## Alibi

He offers his evening in more detail than was asked for, and offers it again if
the conversation goes quiet. A man from Norwich has a great deal to say about
where he has been.

## The Question He Asked

He asked the steward whether the train stops between halts. Told that it does
not, he said nothing further, and did not look surprised.

## Under Enquiry

The lock on his case was forced from the inside.

## The Body

Heavier than a grain factor of Norwich has any business being, and he went down
where he stood rather than where he was moving. Whatever he was asked, he had
stopped to answer it.

The question he had been putting to people all evening he did not get to put
again.
