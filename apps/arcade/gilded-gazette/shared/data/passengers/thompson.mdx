A grain factor from Norwich, travelling on business he describes twice and
differently.

## Appearance

A good coat kept too long, boots newly resoled, and a case with a lock that has
been forced and repaired.

## Alibi

He dined early, retired early, and slept through it, which is what a man says
when he has nothing to hide and also when he has.

- Says he was asleep by ten
- Says he heard the guard and nothing before it
- Says he has never travelled this line before

## The Question He Asked

He asked the steward whether the train stops between halts. Told that it does
not, he said nothing further, and did not look surprised.

## Under Enquiry

The lock on his case was forced from the inside.
