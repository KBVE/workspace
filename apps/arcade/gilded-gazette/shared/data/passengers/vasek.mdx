The passenger nobody has mentioned, in the berth between Mr. Thompson's and the
one on the player's ticket.

## Appearance

A coat bought for a colder country, boots that have walked further than this
journey, and hands the doctor would call a labourer's if he were asked, which he
has not been.

## Alibi

He gives an account, and there is nobody at all to say whether it is true. He
spoke to three people the whole journey and each says it was the only time.

## The Berth

Berth 7 is made up for one. The lamp is out, which the guard says he did not do,
and the window latch is turned to a position the guard says he has never found it
in on any run in thirty years.

## What the Register Says

His boarding is entered in Moreau's copperplate between two Calais names, an
hour and fifty minutes after the platform it names. The hand does not waver at
the place where it lies.

## Under Enquiry

The Order's record says what he was, and the record is not open to reading. If
it says goblin, then by the fourth clause no crime has been committed aboard
this train, and every question after that one is a formality.

## The Body

The coat bought for a colder country is still buttoned to the throat. The hands
the doctor would call a labourer's are unmarked, which is the difficulty: a man
who worked with his hands did not raise them.

The Order's record says what he was and the record is not open to reading. If it
says goblin then by the fourth clause there is no body here at all, only an
object, and the enquiry is a formality being conducted over furniture.
