The only passenger aboard since Paris, and the only one the guard greets by
name.

## Appearance

Travelling clothes worn comfortable, a sample case he never opens, and the calm
of a man who has made this run forty times.

## Alibi

Whatever account he gives, the steward confirms it without being asked, and that
is the trouble with it. A man known to the staff by name is a man whose evening
comes with witnesses already prepared.

## The Appointment Book

Tonight's page carries a single entry at half past eight, in a hand that is not
his, and the page before it has been removed.

## Under Enquiry

He is the only passenger with no reason to be nervous, and he is nervous.

## The Body

Comfortable, the way a man is who has made the run forty times, and the calm has
not entirely left him. The appointment book was under him rather than on him,
which takes putting there.

Tonight's page is still the page it was open at. The page before it is still
missing.
