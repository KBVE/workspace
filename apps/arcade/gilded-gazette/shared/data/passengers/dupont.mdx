The only passenger aboard since Paris, and the only one the guard greets by
name.

## Appearance

Travelling clothes worn comfortable, a sample case he never opens, and the calm
of a man who has made this run forty times.

## Alibi

He was in the dining car until the service closed, which the steward confirms
without being asked, and which is the trouble with it.

- Says he dined at eight, as always
- Says he waited for a colleague who did not come
- Says he noticed nothing unusual

## The Appointment Book

Tonight's page carries a single entry at half past eight, in a hand that is not
his, and the page before it has been removed.

## Under Enquiry

He is the only passenger with no reason to be nervous, and he is nervous.
