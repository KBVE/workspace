A courier for a London bank, chained to a case at the wrist, who reserved a
berth at Calais and has not once been in it.

## Appearance

A plain suit that has been slept in and not slept in, a chain at the cuff, and
the flat readiness of a man who has done this leg before and expects it to be
the one.

## Alibi

Whatever he says he was doing, he was doing it standing, awake, and where he
could see a door. That is either a professional habit or the description of
somebody who knew what was coming.

## The Case

Locked, chained, and never once set on the floor. He has been offered the
guard's van for it twice, by two different people, and has refused both without
appearing to consider it.

## Under Enquiry

He paid for a berth he has not used, which means there is a made-up bed aboard
this train that nobody has been in and nobody has been watching.

## The Body

The chain is still at the wrist and the case is still on the chain, which is the
first thing anybody checked and the wrong thing to have found.

Whatever this was, it was not the case. He was facing whoever it was, the way he
has faced every door since Calais.
