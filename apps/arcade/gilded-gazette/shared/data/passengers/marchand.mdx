An order that has not existed on any map since the crusades, travelling first
class with a crate nobody is permitted to open.

## Appearance

A travelling cloak over mail that shows at the wrist, and the flat calm of
someone who has been in a fight and expects another.

## Alibi

Her account is whatever the oath permits her to give, and no part of it can be
checked by anybody aboard. She offers it without impatience, as though the
impossibility of confirming it were somebody else's difficulty.

## The Warrant

Sealed in wax that is not any nation's, countersigned by a house that fell four
hundred years ago, and honoured at Calais without a word.

## Under Enquiry

The only passenger armed, the only passenger with a reason to be, and the only
one whose story cannot be broken because nothing about it can be checked.

## The Body

Under the travelling cloak the mail is intact, unpierced, and did not fail. She
was not killed through her armour, which narrows it to somebody she let close
enough not to need to be.

The guard's van key is not on her, and it was never off her.
