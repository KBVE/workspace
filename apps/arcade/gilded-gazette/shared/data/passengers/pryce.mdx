The man who confirms everybody else's evening, and whose own nobody has thought
to ask about.

## Appearance

The company's white jacket, pressed this morning and still pressed, and the flat
attentive face of somebody who has spent twenty years being spoken past.

## Alibi

Whatever account he gives is the account of a man who was working, which is the
one alibi aboard that explains being everywhere. He has not been pressed on it,
because he is the one being asked about everybody else.

## What He Pours

He remembers what every passenger drinks and volunteers it before he is asked,
which has so far been taken for helpfulness. He has not once been asked what he
himself had, and the answer is that the service brandy is three measures down.

## Under Enquiry

He is the only person aboard whose testimony is taken as corroboration rather
than as evidence, and the difference has not been examined.

## The Body

In the white jacket, pressed this morning and no longer pressed. The cloth over
one arm is folded the way he folds it, which means he was working when it
stopped.

Nobody thought to ask him anything all evening, and now there is a queue of
questions with nowhere to go.
