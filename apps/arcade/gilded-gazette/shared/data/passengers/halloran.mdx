A company engineer nobody sent for, riding on a pass that lets him aboard any
train on the line and obliges him to explain himself on none of them.

## Appearance

Working clothes under a good coat that does not belong with them, and hands that
have been cleaned recently and thoroughly enough to be remarked on.

## Alibi

Whatever he says he was doing, it is company business, and company business is
the one account aboard that cannot be checked by anybody riding as a passenger.

## The Toolbag

He has carried it since Calais and has not opened it. A man who is aboard to
work opens his bag; a man who is aboard to be seen carrying one does not.

## Under Enquiry

He is the only person aboard whose name is on no list -- not the manifest, not
the register, not the reservation book. The pass is real. The company has no
record of issuing it to anybody travelling tonight.

## The Body

In the good coat rather than the working clothes, which is the wrong way round
for a man who says he was working. The hands are clean.

The toolbag is beside him, and it is open, and there is nothing in it that a
railwayman would carry.
