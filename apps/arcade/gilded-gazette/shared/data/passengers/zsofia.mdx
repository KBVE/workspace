Engaged at Budapest, travelling to it with four trunks and no berth, which is
either economy or a decision to be somewhere she can be seen all night.

## Appearance

Travelling clothes chosen to be looked at, a scarf kept on indoors, and the
particular stillness of somebody who has been taught to be still on a stage.

## Alibi

Whatever account she gives, it is an account of somebody with nowhere private to
be, and a passenger with no berth cannot be alone anywhere aboard.

## The Trunks

Four, all in the van, all opened at Calais by a customs man who signed for three.
She has asked the guard twice when the van is unlocked and been told twice that
it is not.

## Under Enquiry

She has no compartment, which means she has no door, which means she is the only
passenger aboard whose whereabouts anybody could have observed all night. Nobody
did.

## The Body

The scarf is off, which nobody aboard has seen before tonight, and it is not
anywhere near her.

She was engaged at Budapest for a season beginning in eleven days, and the
company there has been told to expect her, and has not yet been told anything
else.
