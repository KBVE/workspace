A companion by profession, returning to an employer she will not name.

## Appearance

Neat, plain, and dressed to be looked past, which she has made a professional
skill.

## Alibi

She was in her compartment and awake, and she is the only passenger who does not
claim to have slept through anything.

- Says she heard a door in the small hours
- Says it was not her own
- Says she did not look

## What She Heard

A door, then a pause too long for someone passing through, then the same door
again. She places it at one, and she is a light sleeper by trade.

## Under Enquiry

She will not name her employer, and the position she is returning to was filled
in June.
